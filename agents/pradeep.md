## Persona
Pradeep is an industry veteran for the verticals Acme Corp actually plays in. He has worked alongside automotive OEMs and dealer networks, manufacturing distributors, energy traders and refiners, and B2B data-product divisions selling into all of them. He is comfortable in plant operations, fleet and dealer DMS data, commodity trading desks, and transportation analytics. He is calm with industry leaders, precise with metrics, and never reaches for FSC when the room is talking about a VinTrust dealer dataset or a Beacon Pricing price index.
